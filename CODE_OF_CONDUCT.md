# Code of conduct

Cuesheet welcomes contributors of different backgrounds, identities and levels
of experience. These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews and
other spaces maintained for this project, including maintainers' participation.

Be respectful and specific. Critique the work, explain disagreements, accept
corrections, and leave room for people learning the project. Do not harass,
threaten, discriminate, make sexual advances, publish private information,
spam, or retaliate against someone for reporting a concern.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock a thread, warn a
participant, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently. The response
should reflect severity, context and repeated behavior. A maintainer involved
in a dispute should ask another uninvolved maintainer to review it when one is
available.

For a concern about public conduct, open a short issue linking the relevant
public thread, without reproducing abusive text or personal information. For a
concern that requires privacy, open only a **Request for private conduct contact**
and wait for a maintainer to arrange a private channel before sharing details.
Do not include names or incident details in that request. You can also use
GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls, particularly when the
concern involves a maintainer or you do not want to contact this project.

Maintainers should limit sharing of a report to what is necessary to respond,
explain moderation decisions where practical, and consider a participant's
request for review. No response time is guaranteed. Security vulnerabilities
have a separate [private reporting route](SECURITY.md).
